🚗 Getting There

🚗 Rental Car (Recommended)

Porto Airport → Douro Valley is ~1.5 hours via the A4/IP4. Pick up at OPO, drop in Lisbon. The N222 road from Régua to Pinhão is one of the world's most scenic drives. Essential for quinta-hopping.

🚂 Train (Douro Line)

Porto São Bento → Peso da Régua (€8–10, 2h) or Pinhão (€11, 2.5h). Scenic riverside route. Limited schedules — check cp.pt. Many quintas are walkable from Pinhão station.

🚢 River Cruise

Day cruises from Porto to Régua/Pinhão (~€50–80pp) with lunch & tastings included. A relaxing way to arrive. Return by train. Book via dourovalley-tours.com.
